Market making

Separate $500 paper bankroll. Rests bids on Polymarket US (earning the maker rebate) priced so bid + Kalshi hedge + fees stays under $0.995, then hedges on Kalshi when filled. A fill counts only when volume trades at or through our price after everyone ahead of us in line. The least certain of the three tests: real queues and reactions can't be fully simulated.

One-site bundles

How the paper fills work: the scanner reads both order books for every matched pair, then walks the price levels until the next pair would cost more than $0.995 incl. both sites' fees (Kalshi 0.07·p·(1−p), Polymarket US 0.0695·p·(1−p)), capped at $150 per pair and each site's $900 paper cash. Limits: the two books are read a fraction of a second apart, nobody else is assumed to take the price first, and in-game markets are skipped. A gap over 15¢ is treated as a wrong match and ignored. The laptop publishes a fresh snapshot about once a minute; this page re-reads it every 90 seconds.
